below by authority ID; compiled decisions combine those observance authorities
with any general rule authority.

## GOARCH dated observations

Digital Chant Stand's generated Vespers service for the civil evening of
2026-07-25 (liturgical date 2026-07-26, St Paraskevi) has six resurrectional
stichera followed by four for the saint. It then supplies a Menaion `Glory` for
St Paraskevi and an Octoechos `Both now` Theotokion in plagal second mode.

Source: <https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/h/s/2026/07/25/ve/en/index.html>

The generated service for the civil evening of 2026-08-01 (liturgical date
2026-08-02, transfer of St Stephen's relic) likewise has six resurrectional
stichera followed by four for the saint. It supplies the saint's `Glory` and an
Octoechos `Both now` Theotokion in plagal fourth mode.

Source: <https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/h/s/2026/08/01/ve/en/index.html>

Both fixtures are labeled `observed_behavior`. DCS is production evidence, not
the asserted authority explaining the underlying rubric. The rank string and
rule shape remain experimental pending review against rubrical sources and a
knowledgeable priest.

## OCA ordinary-Sunday cases

The OCA Department of Liturgical Music and Translations' outline for Great
Vespers at a Sunday vigil, outside a forefeast or afterfeast, states:

- a lesser saint uses 7 resurrectional + 3 saint stichera;
- a six-stichera saint uses 6 resurrectional + 4 saint stichera;
- `Glory` is for the saint when prescribed;
- `Now` is the dogmatikon in the tone of the week.

Source: <https://www.oca.org/liturgics/outlines/great-vespers-at-vigil-sun>

The dated OCA order for Sunday, 2023-08-27 instantiates the lesser-saint case
for Venerable Pimen the Great in Tone 3: seven resurrectional stichera, three
for the saint, the saint's `Glory`, and the Tone 3 dogmatikon at `Now`.

Source: <https://www.oca.org/PDF/Music/Rubrics/2023-0827-order-services.docx>

The dated OCA order for Sunday, 2026-09-06 instantiates the six-stichera case
for the Miracle of the Archangel Michael at Colossae in Tone 5: six
resurrectional stichera, four for the commemoration, its `Glory`, and the Tone
5 dogmatikon at `Now`.

Source: <https://www.oca.org/PDF/Music/Rubrics/2026-0906-order-services.docx>

Both official DOCX files were rendered to five pages with LibreOffice 26.2.5.2
and Poppler 26.02.0-0. All ten rendered pages were inspected at original detail
to verify the cited service-order text and document integrity.

The OCA rank glossary separately identifies `Six Stichera` and `Ordinary Day`
as distinct rank classes:
<https://www.oca.org/liturgics/outlines/classes-of-feasts>.

The fixture rank assignments are grounded in the dated service orders. They
remain engineering interpretations of OCA publications, not ecclesiastical
approval of this experimental pack.
