## GOARCH: 2026-07-25 evening / 2026-07-26 liturgical day

Digital Chant Stand's generated Vespers service for St Paraskevi visibly has
six resurrectional stichera followed by four for the saint. It then supplies a
Menaion `Glory` for St Paraskevi and an Octoechos `Both now` Theotokion in the
mode of the saint's doxastikon.

Source: <https://dcs.goarch.org/goa/dcs/h/s/2026/07/25/ve/en/index.html>

This fixture is labeled `observed_behavior`. DCS is production evidence, not
the asserted authority explaining the underlying rule. The rank string and
rule shape remain experimental pending review against rubrical sources and a
knowledgeable priest.

## OCA: ordinary Sunday cases

The OCA Department of Liturgical Music and Translations' outline for Great
Vespers at a Sunday vigil, outside a forefeast or afterfeast, states:

- a lesser saint uses 7 resurrectional + 3 saint stichera;
- a six-stichera saint uses 6 resurrectional + 4 saint stichera;
- `Glory` is for the saint when prescribed;
- `Now` is the dogmatikon in the tone of the week.

Source: <https://www.oca.org/liturgics/outlines/great-vespers-at-vigil-sun>

The OCA rank glossary separately identifies `Six Stichera` and `Ordinary Day`
as distinct rank classes:
<https://www.oca.org/liturgics/outlines/classes-of-feasts>.

The two OCA observances are explicitly named context fixtures and have no fixed
dates. They test the published conditional rubric without making unsupported
calendar or saint-rank claims.
